Gypsy Jazz Hub

I built a home for our community — jam sessions, musicians, concerts and festivals on a world map. Find a jam near you.

Gypsy jazz is more than music. It is a living community of players, nights, festivals, and the people who show up.

gypsyjazzhub.com puts that map in one place: where to jam this week, who is playing, camps and workshops, history and news — from Paris to Brooklyn, Amsterdam to Osaka.

  • Interactive globe of jams and events by country
  • Upcoming sessions with venues and times
  • Learn, camps, history, and community news
  • Built for players and listeners who travel with the music
